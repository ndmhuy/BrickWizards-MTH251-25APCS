\subsection{Problem 8. Related rates: moving shadow}
A lamp is mounted at the top of a 5-m high pole. A person who is 1.8 m tall walks away from the base of the pole along a straight path at a speed of $1.2\text{ m/s}$. Assume the lamp is a point source.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Draw a diagram and introduce notation for the distance $x(t)$ of the person from the pole and the length $s(t)$ of the shadow on the ground.
    \item Use similar triangles to derive a relationship between $x$ and $s$.
    \item Use related rates to find $\dfrac{ds}{dt}$ when $x=10\text{ m}$. Interpret the sign and magnitude of $\dfrac{ds}{dt}$.
    \item Determine the speed of the tip of the shadow at the same moment, and compare it with the speed of the person.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Strategy}
\begin{itemize}

    \item[(1)] 
    Let $x$ be the horizontal distance of the person from the pole, and let 
    $s$ be the length of the person’s shadow on the ground.  
    Then the tip of the shadow is $x+s$ meters from the pole.

    \item[(2)]
    Observe that the lamp at height $5$ m, the person of height $1.8$ m, and the
    shadow tip form two similar right triangles.  
    Using the ratio of corresponding sides, we set up the proportion
    \[
        \frac{5}{x+s} = \frac{1.8}{s}.
    \]
    Solving this proportionality will give a direct linear relation between 
    the shadow length $s$ and the distance $x$.

    \item[(3)]
    Once the relationship $s = kx$ is obtained (where $k=0.5625$), 
    differentiate with respect to time $t$ to find 
    $\dfrac{ds}{dt}$ in terms of $\dfrac{dx}{dt}$, using the fact that the 
    person walks away from the pole at $1.2$ m/s.

    \item[(4)]
    To determine how fast the \emph{tip} of the shadow moves, 
    note that its position is $x+s$.  
    Differentiate $x+s$ with respect to time and substitute 
    $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=1.2$ and the relation between $s$ and $x$ 
    to obtain $\dfrac{d(x+s)}{dt}$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Solution}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.9]

  % Ground line
  \draw[->] (-0.5,0) -- (7,0) node[right]{ground};

  % Pole and lamp
  \draw[very thick] (0,0) -- (0,5);
  \fill (0,5) circle (2pt);
  \node[above] at (0,5) {lamp};
  \draw[<->] (-0.4,0) -- (-0.4,5) node[midway,left] {$5\,\text{m}$};

  % Person
  \draw[very thick] (3,0) -- (3,1.8);
  \fill (3,1.8) circle (1.5pt);
  \node[above] at (3,1.8) {person};
  \draw[<->] (2.6,0) -- (2.6,1.8) node[midway,left] {$1.8\,\text{m}$};

  % Shadow tip
  \coordinate (Tip) at (4.6875,0);
  \fill (Tip) circle (1.5pt);
  \node[below] at (5,0) {shadow tip};

  % Light rays
  \draw[thick] (0,5) -- (Tip);
  \draw[thick,dashed] (0,5) -- (3,1.8);

  % Horizontal distances x and s
  \draw[<->] (0,-0.5) -- (3,-0.5) node[midway,below] {$x$};
  \draw[<->] (3,-1.0) -- (5,-1.0) node[midway,below] {$s$};

\end{tikzpicture}

\begin{itemize}

    \item[(a)] 
    We denote by $x$ the distance of the person from the pole and by $s$ the 
    length of the shadow behind the person. The tip of the shadow is therefore 
    $x+s$ meters from the pole.

    \item[(b)] 
    Using similar triangles, the lamp, person, and shadow satisfy
    \[
        \frac{5}{x+s} = \frac{1.8}{s}.
    \]
    Cross-multiplying gives
    \[
        5s = 1.8(x+s),
    \]
    \[
        3.2s = 1.8x,
    \]
    so the relationship is
    \[
        \boxed{s = \frac{1.8}{3.2}x = 0.5625\,x}.
    \]

    \item[(c)] 
    Differentiate the relation $s = 0.5625x$:
    \[
        \frac{ds}{dt} = 0.5625 \frac{dx}{dt}.
    \]
    Since the person walks at $\frac{dx}{dt} = 1.2$ m/s,
    \[
        \frac{ds}{dt} = 0.5625 * 1.2 = 0.675\ \text{m/s}.
    \]
    The positive sign means the shadow is growing.

    \item[(d)]
    The tip of the shadow is located at
    \[
        x+s = x + 0.5625x = 1.5625x.
    \]
    Differentiating,
    \[
        \frac{d}{dt}(x+s) = 1.5625\frac{dx}{dt}.
    \]
    Substituting $\frac{dx}{dt}=1.2$,
    \[
        \frac{d(x+s)}{dt} = 1.875\ \text{m/s}.
    \]
    Thus, the shadow’s tip moves faster than the person (1.875 m/s compared to 1.2 m/s).

\end{itemize}
